CompTIA SecAI+ (CY0-001) Bootcamp

Two free tools built to prep for the SecAI+ exam, cross-checked against the official Sybex/Wiley study guide by Mike Chapple and Fred Nwanganga. No login, no tracking beyond your own browser's local storage — everything runs client-side.

15 SUB-OBJECTIVES · 83-QUESTION EXAM BANK · UPDATED FROM OFFICIAL STUDY GUIDE
Interactive Console

5-Day Readiness Bootcamp

A self-paced, day-by-day console covering all four exam domains with knowledge checks built in.

  • 47 lecture/lab blocks across a Mon–Fri, 8-hrs/day structure
  • 98-card flashcard deck, 14-pair attack↔control matching drill
  • 60-question timed final exam simulation, freshly drawn each attempt from an 83-question bank
  • Progress auto-saves in your browser — close it and pick up where you left off

Watch & Listen

Narrated Video Course

Same material, spoken-lecture format — for listening while driving, working out, or away from a screen.

  • 18 episodes, one per exam sub-objective (plus intro and closing strategy)
  • Captions highlight in sync as each line is narrated
  • Play/pause, scrub, playback speed, voice picker, autoplay
  • Uses your device's built-in text-to-speech — no audio files to download
